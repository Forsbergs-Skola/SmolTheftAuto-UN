# Smol Thelft Auto — My Contributions

## Overview
In this project I contributed primarily to **UI design**, **full narrative creation**, and **world building**.  
I also created custom assets, organized prefabs, integrated menu/gameplay audio, and supported workflow testing throughout development.

---

## UI Design & Implementation
- Designed and implemented the dialogue UI, HUD elements, quest UI, and inventory layout  
- Created custom Kenney-style assets (icons, borders, sunglasses, snowflakes)  
- Built responsive UI layouts with correct anchors, scaling, and readability  
- Designed the main menu layout and overall presentation  
- Replaced placeholder sprites and maintained a consistent visual identity  

---

## Full Narrative Creation & Writing
- Created the entire story concept for *Smol Thelft Auto* from scratch  
- Wrote all character dialogues used in the game  
- Developed the personalities, humor, and tone of every NPC  
- Structured the quest narrative and how each part connects  
- Adapted the story direction to fit gameplay and project scope  
- Ensured the overall narrative felt cohesive, fun, and aligned with the team's vision  

---

## World Building & Map Design
- Built and decorated the entire overworld map  
- Placed buildings, props, lights, and snow-themed details  
- Designed three quest zones with simple visual storytelling cues  
- Added colliders and ensured correct navigation for players and NPCs
